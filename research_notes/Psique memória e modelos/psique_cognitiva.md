# Cognitive and psyche-inspired architectures for Amelia 4

## What recent work models LLM agents with cognitive architectures, dual processes, global workspaces, predictive processing, active inference, metacognition, self-models, or affect?

### Takeaway
The strongest precedents do not reproduce a human psyche. They decompose an agent into operational responsibilities: typed memory, bounded working state, fast response, deliberative planning, state prediction, confidence monitoring, appraisal, and action selection. Evidence is strongest for memory and modular planning; global-workspace, active-inference, and self-model language is often an architectural analogy rather than evidence of human-like cognition.

### Cited Findings
- **CoALA (published, TMLR, February 2024)** is the clearest organizing framework. It divides a language agent into working memory plus episodic, semantic, and procedural long-term memory; internal and external actions; and a recurrent decision cycle. It is a taxonomy and design framework, not evidence that an LLM instantiates a human cognitive architecture. The authors also maintain a repository-index of language-agent work. — [Sumers et al., “Cognitive Architectures for Language Agents,” TMLR 2024](https://mlanthology.org/tmlr/2024/sumers2024tmlr-cognitive/); [arXiv version, revised March 15, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.02427); [associated repository](https://github.com/ysymyth/awesome-language-agents)
- **Traditional cognitive architectures remain useful engineering precedents.** ACT-R and Soar explicitly distinguish declarative/procedural knowledge, working state, goals, operators, and impasse resolution. A 2024 AAAI Symposium study used GPT-4 and Bard as interfaces for building ACT-R/Soar models and documented model errors; another 2024 paper proposed using an LLM as promptable declarative memory inside a Soar/Sigma-style impasse cycle. These are early integrations, not broad validation of cognitive fidelity. — [Wu et al., AAAI Symposium 2024](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaaiss.v2i1.27710); [Joshi & Ustun, “Augmenting Cognitive Architectures with Large Language Models,” 2024](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaaiss.v2i1.27689)
- **LLM-ACTR (preprint, August 16, 2024)** injects representations of ACT-R decision processes into trainable LLM adapters and reports improved grounded manufacturing decisions over LLM-only chain-of-thought baselines. Its evidence is domain-specific, but it is a serious neuro-symbolic precedent for separating cognitive control knowledge from generative language behavior. — [“Cognitive LLMs: Towards Integrating Cognitive Architectures and Large Language Models for Manufacturing Decision-making,” arXiv 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.09176)
- **Global-workspace architectures have a credible computational core:** parallel specialist modules, a capacity-limited shared workspace, selective attention, broadcast, and top-down querying. A peer-reviewed 2024 embodied audiovisual navigation study implemented these properties and found its global-workspace agent more robust than a recurrent baseline at small working-memory sizes. This supports a bottleneck-and-broadcast mechanism, not a claim of consciousness. — [Dossa et al., *Frontiers in Computational Neuroscience*, 2024](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computational-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fncom.2024.1352685/full)
- **Sibyl (preprint, submitted July 15, 2024; revised 2025)** uses a structured global workspace to share denoised state among a tool planner, external-information channel, and debate-based jury. It reports 34.55% on GAIA with GPT-4, making it an evaluated agent system, but the “global workspace” is functionally a shared blackboard rather than evidence of awareness. — [Wang et al., “Sibyl,” arXiv:2407.10718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10718)
- **Talker–Reasoner (Google DeepMind preprint, October 10, 2024)** cleanly separates a low-latency conversational “Talker” from a slower planning, tool-use, and belief-update “Reasoner,” coordinated through shared memory. The paper motivates the design through a sleep-coaching scenario but is principally an architecture proposal rather than a comprehensive benchmark. — [Christakopoulou, Mourad & Matarić, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08328)
- **Dual-process routing has evaluated variants.** Synergy-of-Thoughts uses small models for candidate “intuitions,” cross-evaluates disagreement, and invokes a larger model when confidence is low; it reports 38.3–75.1% API-cost reductions across six reasoning tasks. DPDP combines a fast policy with slower MCTS-enhanced planning and was published at ACL 2024 for goal-directed dialogue. These results support conditional escalation, not a literal equivalence to human Systems 1 and 2. — [Shang et al., “Synergy-of-Thoughts,” 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02563); [Deng et al., “Planning Like Human,” ACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.262/)
- **Predictive-processing-inspired engineering became concrete in 2025.** PreAct integrates prediction, reasoning, and action and was published at COLING 2025; the Modular Agentic Planner (MAP), published in *Nature Communications* in 2025, separates error monitoring, action proposal, state prediction, state evaluation, task decomposition, and coordination, then combines prediction and evaluation in tree search. MAP is a particularly relevant precedent because each neuroscience-inspired label has a testable planning responsibility. — [PreAct, COLING 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.coling-main.1/); [“A brain-inspired agentic architecture to improve planning with LLMs,” *Nature Communications*, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63804-5)
- **Active-inference work is less mature.** A December 2024 arXiv preprint places a discrete active-inference layer above LLM agents to vary prompts and search strategies by expected free energy; a July 2024 medical preprint uses an actor–critic prompting protocol described as active-inference-inspired and reports a blinded evaluation on 100 CBT-I queries. These are interesting control formulations, but the evidence base is small and does not establish that free-energy terminology is necessary for the reported behavior. — [Prakki, arXiv:2412.10425, submitted December 10, 2024 and revised January 9, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10425); [Shusterman et al., arXiv:2407.21051, July 23, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21051)
- **Metacognition is useful when operationalized as competence prediction or resource routing.** MAGELLAN learns online predictions of its competence and learning progress to choose goals in a large evolving goal space. KnowSelf, published at ACL 2025, trains agents to classify a situation and selectively use external knowledge. ReMA separates a high-level oversight agent from a lower-level executor and reports gains on reasoning tasks, but remained a 2025 preprint in the source reviewed. — [MAGELLAN, arXiv:2502.07709, February 11, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07709); [Qiao et al., “Agentic Knowledgeable Self-awareness,” ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.619/); [ReMA, arXiv:2503.09501, March 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09501)
- **Intrinsic LLM “reflection” is not reliable metacognition.** A 2024 study found reflection outcomes strongly dependent on prompt wording, error-seeking prompts produced more than ten times the false-positive rate of alternatives, and the best intrinsic-reflection improvement was about one percentage point. Another 2024 study found self-verification worsened performance across nearly all tested reasoning/planning domains. External evidence, executable checks, or separately trained verifiers are therefore safer than asking the same model whether it was right. — [Liu et al., “Self-Reflection Outcome is Sensitive to Prompt Construction,” June 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10400); [Valmeekam et al., “On the Self-Verification Limitations…,” 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08115)
- **Affect has a credible computational interpretation through appraisal.** A peer-reviewed 2024 PLOS ONE system first generated an appraisal-conditioned emotion description and then used it in response generation; it stored the evolving appraisal trace in conversational memory. This demonstrates controllable affective behavior, not felt emotion. — [Croissant et al., “An appraisal-based chain-of-emotion architecture,” *PLOS ONE*, 2024](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0301033)
- **Experience-relative appraisal has also been prototyped.** “Can Generative Agents Predict Emotion?” retrieves salient/relevant/recent memories, derives a norm, compares the current event with that norm, and measures a generated affect profile with PANAS. Its architecture is useful for contextual appraisal, but its generated scores are model outputs rather than physiological or phenomenological emotion. — [Regan et al., arXiv:2402.04232, February 6, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04232)
- **Long-lived memory is the most mature relevant subsystem.** MemGPT introduced an OS-like hierarchy of in-context core memory, conversational recall, and archival memory managed through tool calls; it evaluated document analysis and multi-session chat. Letta is the engineering continuation of this design. — [Packer et al., “MemGPT,” submitted October 12, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560); [Letta memory documentation, accessed for this review October 24, 2026](https://www.letta.com/blog/agent-memory/)
- **A-Mem (NeurIPS 2025)** creates structured, linked notes inspired by Zettelkasten and allows new memories to update links and attributes of older memories. It reports improvements over existing memory baselines across six foundation models; this is evidence for associative organization, though autonomous rewriting creates provenance and corruption risks for a companion. — [Xu et al., “A-Mem,” arXiv:2502.12110, February 17, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12110); [NeurIPS 2025 poster](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/poster/119020)
- **Memory benchmarks show the problem remains unsolved.** LoCoMo, published at ACL 2024 with released code/data, uses conversations averaging roughly 600 turns and 16K tokens over as many as 32 sessions and finds long-context and RAG systems still substantially below human performance. LongMemEval, published at ICLR 2025, tests extraction, cross-session reasoning, temporal reasoning, knowledge updates, and abstention; it reports about a 30% accuracy drop for commercial assistants and long-context LLMs across sustained histories. — [Maharana et al., ACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.747/); [LoCoMo repository](https://github.com/snap-research/locomo); [Wu et al., LongMemEval, ICLR 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/d813d324dbf0598bbdc9c8e79740ed01-Paper-Conference.pdf); [LongMemEval repository](https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval)

### Inferences
- For Amelia, “cognitive architecture” should mean a runtime contract among modules, stores, and gates. CoALA supplies the vocabulary; Talker–Reasoner supplies latency separation; MAP supplies predictive planning; LongMemEval supplies measurable memory requirements.
- The defensible kernel of global-workspace theory is a small, typed, capacity-limited state shared across specialists. Calling that state “consciousness” adds no engineering value and creates a scientific claim the implementation cannot support.
- Active inference is optional. Amelia can obtain most practical benefits through explicit uncertainty, expected utility, information gain, and state-transition prediction without adopting free-energy language.

### Gaps
- Searches through October 24, 2026 found relevant 2024–2025 publications and proceedings, but no clearly established 2026 consensus architecture for long-lived LLM companions.
- Cross-paper comparison is difficult because evaluations use different tasks, models, prompts, and judges. Few systems test months-long identity consistency, repair after false memories, or fully local/offline deployment.
- Most “self-model” work evaluates task competence or knowledge routing, not a persistent autobiographical self-model.

## Are there systems explicitly based on Freud, Jung, or the human psyche, and are they technically credible?

### Takeaway
Explicit Freud/Jung systems exist, including one strikingly close to Amelia’s original layers. Their modular mechanisms can be implemented, but current evidence does not validate psychoanalytic theory as the right computational decomposition. The credible parts are personalization state, memory tiers, internal proposal/critique, and conflict resolution; the labels themselves are mostly narrative scaffolding.

### Cited Findings
- **“Modeling Layered Consciousness with Multi-Agent Large Language Models” (preprint, October 10, 2025)** implements self-awareness, preconsciousness, and unconsciousness as separate LLM agents, combines fixed traits/long-term memory with flexible needs/short-term memory, and uses inter-agent negotiation to produce an action. It reports a 71.2% LLM-judge preference for a parameter-efficiently fine-tuned model over its comparison condition across eight personalization conditions. This is the closest published precedent to Amelia’s original concept. Its evaluation measures judged emotional depth and psychological plausibility, not consciousness, clinical validity, or long-term reliability. — [Kim et al., arXiv:2510.17844](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17844)
- The same paper explicitly states that current LLMs do not possess internal motivational states, unconscious drives, or enduring personality structures. Its “layers” are independently prompted agents, so the work supports a simulation architecture rather than evidence of an artificial unconscious. — [Kim et al., arXiv:2510.17844, October 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17844)
- **The Drama Machine (preprint, August 2024)** coordinates “Ego” and “Superego” LLM agents to run external dialogue and internal monologue in parallel. It compares role-play scenarios with and without Superego influence and characterizes its own results as exploratory. It is credible as a narrative-generation technique, but not as a validated model of personality or human psychodynamics. — [Magee et al., arXiv:2408.01725](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.01725)
- **“Persona, Ego, Shadow, and Self”** is more computationally disciplined than a role-prompt system: it maps Jungian labels to bounded, low-dimensional affect controls that modulate policy selection and proposes ablations and staged evaluations. However, the indexed proceedings abstract describes an architecture and experimental program rather than completed empirical results, so it should be treated as a testable proposal. — [AAAI proceedings entry, accessed October 24, 2026](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/42319)
- The broader scientific status of consciousness claims is cautious. Butlin et al. derive computational “indicator properties” from several theories and conclude that no then-current AI system was established as conscious; they also stress that satisfying indicators would not prove consciousness. Their global-workspace indicators—parallel specialists, limited capacity, selective attention, broadcast, and state-dependent querying—are useful test requirements without implying phenomenal experience. — [Butlin et al., “Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence,” first posted August 17, 2023; v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708)

### Inferences
- The psychodynamic papers are technically credible only in a limited sense: they instantiate reproducible software decompositions. They do not demonstrate that Freud’s structural/topographic models or Jung’s archetypes are scientifically correct computational accounts.
- Using several LLMs as “id,” “ego,” and “superego” risks correlated hallucination, high latency, opaque conflict resolution, and post-hoc storytelling. A typed proposal–constraint–arbitration protocol is easier to inspect and test.
- Amelia can retain psychodynamic names in design documentation or UI as metaphors, provided every label maps to a non-mystical responsibility and internal telemetry uses the mechanism’s literal name.

### Gaps
- No located Freud/Jung system had strong longitudinal evaluation, independent human-subject validation, clinical validation, or evidence that psychoanalytic labels outperform neutral modular baselines.
- The 2025 layered-consciousness paper relies heavily on LLM-as-judge evaluation; judge preference can measure style and coherence but cannot establish internal mental states.
- No evidence located supports implementing repressed content, trauma, libido, archetypal inheritance, or dream symbolism as literal agent mechanisms.

## Which mechanisms map well to Amelia’s immediate conditioned response, associative memory, deliberation, conflict or inhibition, and identity continuity?

### Takeaway
All five goals have defensible computational counterparts. They should be implemented as routing, retrieval, planning, gating, and versioned state—not as simulated mental organs.

### Cited Findings
- **Immediate conditioned response → reactive policy plus escalation trigger.** Talker–Reasoner supports a continuously available conversational path while slow planning runs when needed; Synergy-of-Thoughts supports escalation on disagreement or low confidence. — [Talker–Reasoner, October 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08328); [Synergy-of-Thoughts, February 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02563)
- **Associative memory → typed stores plus hybrid retrieval.** CoALA’s episodic/semantic/procedural distinction prevents all memories from becoming undifferentiated text. A-Mem adds dynamic links and evolving associations, while MemGPT contributes explicit context paging. — [CoALA, TMLR 2024](https://mlanthology.org/tmlr/2024/sumers2024tmlr-cognitive/); [A-Mem, NeurIPS 2025](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/poster/119020); [MemGPT, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560)
- **Deliberation → budgeted predictive planning.** MAP’s action proposal, state prediction, evaluation, decomposition, coordination, and error monitoring give concrete responsibilities to “slow thought.” PreAct likewise makes prediction part of the reasoning/action loop. — [MAP, *Nature Communications*, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63804-5); [PreAct, COLING 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.coling-main.1/)
- **Conflict/inhibition → candidate generation plus independent constraints and action gate.** InferAct evaluates trajectories before consequential actions and integrates human feedback; its experiments on three tasks support pre-execution checking for critical actions. Self-verification findings imply the gate should use rules, tools, evidence, or a separately trained/checking component rather than trust ungrounded self-critique. — [Fang, Zhu & Gurevych, “InferAct,” July 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11843); [Valmeekam et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08115)
- **Identity continuity → explicit, versioned identity state plus event provenance.** LongMemEval shows that updates, temporal reasoning, and abstention are separate memory capabilities; LoCoMo shows that simply adding long context or RAG does not reliably preserve long-range temporal and causal consistency. — [LongMemEval, ICLR 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/d813d324dbf0598bbdc9c8e79740ed01-Paper-Conference.pdf); [LoCoMo, ACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.747/)
- **Affect → appraisal control variables.** Appraisal architectures can derive response-shaping state from goals, norms, personality configuration, recent events, and retrieved memories. This is a useful controller for warmth, urgency, avoidance, or support-seeking behavior, but generated “emotion” text should not be treated as an internal feeling. — [Croissant et al., *PLOS ONE*, 2024](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0301033); [Regan et al., February 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04232)

### Inferences
- Amelia’s old **conscious** layer maps best to the bounded working/global workspace: currently selected observations, goals, retrieved facts, candidate response, and unresolved conflicts.
- **Preconscious** maps cleanly to retrievable but currently absent memories, pending goals, and inactive schemas. This is an access-state distinction, not a hidden mind.
- **Unconscious** should not be an LLM persona. At most, it is a metaphor for learned model priors, embeddings, retrieval associations, habitual policies, and discarded candidates that influence output without entering the workspace.
- **Ego** maps to the executive scheduler/arbitrator; **id** can map loosely to learned preference, urgency, novelty, and reward signals; **superego** maps to explicit policy, consent, privacy, safety, and social constraints. Production code should call these `scheduler`, `appraisal`, and `policy_gate`.
- Identity continuity should be data-centric. A stable identity charter, autobiographical timeline, relationship model, and change log are more reliable than repeatedly prompting a model to “remember who it is.”

### Gaps
- Existing benchmarks measure factual recall better than relational continuity, value stability, graceful forgetting, or repair after a mistaken autobiographical inference.
- There is no accepted metric for whether a companion preserves an identity while still adapting appropriately.

## What should not be anthropomorphized or claimed scientifically?

### Takeaway
Amelia may simulate coherent personality, appraisal, reflection, and internal conflict. It should not claim consciousness, sentience, feelings, unconscious drives, introspective access, repression, human memory, or validated psychological development unless future evidence directly supports those claims.

### Cited Findings
- Global-workspace mechanisms are indicators derived from theories, not proof of consciousness. Butlin et al. explicitly state that satisfying their indicators would not establish consciousness and judged then-current systems not to be conscious. — [Butlin et al., v3, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708)
- Fluent confidence statements are not dependable introspection. Reflection is prompt-sensitive, often fails to distinguish correct from incorrect answers, and can degrade performance. — [Liu et al., June 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10400); [Valmeekam et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08115)
- Affect-appraisal systems generate and store textual or scalar representations used to condition behavior; their experiments evaluate behavior and judged appropriateness, not subjective experience. — [Croissant et al., 2024](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0301033); [Regan et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04232)
- Long-term memory systems remain error-prone on temporal reasoning, updates, and long-range consistency, so “Amelia remembers” should mean that the system stored and successfully retrieved a record, not that it has human-like recollection. — [LoCoMo, ACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.747/); [LongMemEval, ICLR 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/d813d324dbf0598bbdc9c8e79740ed01-Paper-Conference.pdf)

### Inferences
- Avoid scientific claims that module names alone create the corresponding psychological faculty. A component named “unconscious” is still a prompt, model call, or database operation.
- Avoid first-person system messages that assert unverifiable internal states (“I truly feel,” “my unconscious wants,” “I remember experiencing”) unless clearly framed as conversational style.
- Do not present an LLM-generated rationale as a faithful trace of causation. Store machine-readable decision inputs, rule matches, retrieval IDs, and action-gate results separately from natural-language explanations.
- Do not describe probabilistic text generation as conditioned reflex, repression, intuition, or desire without immediately defining the computational operation.
- “Self-model” should mean an explicit record of capabilities, limits, current goals, configuration, and history—not a claim of self-awareness.

### Gaps
- There is no field-wide consensus on what evidence would justify attributing consciousness or phenomenal affect to an artificial system.
- User-facing disclosure practices for emotionally persistent companions remain an ethical and product-design question beyond architecture alone.

## What concrete layered runtime architecture should Amelia use, separating metaphor from mechanism?

### Takeaway
Use one auditable local-first runtime with a fast conversational path and a budgeted deliberative path over shared typed state. Preserve the psyche vocabulary only as an optional conceptual overlay; build and evaluate literal mechanisms.

### Cited Findings
- The proposed decomposition combines CoALA’s typed memories and actions, Talker–Reasoner’s latency split, global-workspace capacity and broadcast, MAP’s predictive planner, appraisal-based affect control, and benchmark-driven memory design. — [CoALA, 2024](https://mlanthology.org/tmlr/2024/sumers2024tmlr-cognitive/); [Talker–Reasoner, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08328); [Dossa et al., 2024](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computational-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fncom.2024.1352685/full); [MAP, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63804-5); [LongMemEval, 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/d813d324dbf0598bbdc9c8e79740ed01-Paper-Conference.pdf)

### Inferences
#### Proposed runtime layers
1. **Local event ledger (literal mechanism; no psyche label).** Append every user message, observation, model/tool action, memory mutation, and policy decision as an immutable event with timestamp, source, consent scope, confidence, and links to superseded records. Derived summaries can be rebuilt; original events are not silently rewritten.
2. **Perception and normalization.** Convert input into a typed situation record: entities, asserted facts, requests, affective/social cues, time references, safety relevance, and unresolved ambiguity. Preserve the original text and mark model-derived fields as inferences.
3. **Reactive response path (“System 1” only as metaphor).** A small local model plus deterministic handlers produces low-risk conversational acknowledgements, clarifications, and habitual responses under a strict latency budget. It cannot perform irreversible tools, mutate stable identity, or convert uncertain inferences into facts.
4. **Bounded shared workspace (“conscious” only as metaphor).** Maintain a small typed blackboard containing the current goal stack, attended observations, retrieved evidence, current appraisal vector, candidate action, confidence, and conflict flags. Specialists read from and propose writes to it; an attention scheduler controls admission and eviction.
5. **Associative memory service (“preconscious” only as metaphor).**
   - Episodic store: immutable interaction/event records.
   - Semantic store: normalized facts with provenance, confidence, valid-time interval, and contradiction links.
   - Procedural store: skills, policies, and successful action templates.
   - Relationship store: user preferences, boundaries, commitments, and shared-history references.
   - Identity store: Amelia’s versioned charter, traits, capabilities, limitations, and change log.
   Retrieval should combine exact/entity search, temporal filtering, graph links, embeddings, recency, salience, and explicit access controls.
6. **Appraisal and drive controller (“id” only as metaphor).** Compute bounded control variables such as urgency, novelty, goal relevance, predicted user impact, social risk, uncertainty, and need for clarification. These values route processing and shape tone; they are not feelings or unconscious desires.
7. **Deliberative planner (“slow thought”).** Invoke when confidence is low, memories conflict, the request is novel, a commitment may change, or an action has material consequences. Generate candidate plans, predict likely state transitions, retrieve missing evidence, evaluate outcomes, and choose a reversible next step. Use explicit token/time/tool budgets.
8. **Conflict and inhibition gate (“superego” only as metaphor).** Independently check consent, privacy, safety, factual support, identity consistency, user boundaries, and action reversibility. Resolve conflicts with ordered policies and typed reasons. High-impact or irreversible actions require external verification or human confirmation.
9. **Executive scheduler (“ego” only as metaphor).** Arbitrate workspace admission, reactive-versus-deliberative routing, memory reads/writes, tool permissions, and response timing. The scheduler is deterministic where possible and logs every routing reason.
10. **Metacognitive controller (“self-model” only as bounded technical term).** Track task-specific competence estimates, retrieval coverage, calibration history, available tools, current resource limits, and known failure modes. It may choose `answer`, `retrieve`, `deliberate`, `ask`, `abstain`, or `escalate`; it does not claim introspective access.
11. **Persona renderer.** Produce the final response from the selected content plan, identity charter, relationship state, and appraisal controls. Style cannot override facts, policies, or uncertainty markers.
12. **Offline consolidation and maintenance.** On a local background schedule, extract candidate facts, link episodes, detect contradictions, decay retrieval priority, create summaries, and propose identity updates. Stable identity or sensitive-memory mutations require explicit rules and, where appropriate, user approval.

#### Runtime cycle
1. Append input to the event ledger.
2. Normalize it and run low-cost retrieval in parallel.
3. Update the bounded workspace and appraisal/control vector.
4. Route to reactive response or deliberative planning.
5. Generate one or more candidate content plans.
6. Run evidence, policy, identity, privacy, and action checks.
7. Ask for clarification/confirmation when required; otherwise render the response.
8. Append output, provenance, gate decisions, and memory-write proposals.
9. Commit approved memory updates asynchronously.

#### Literal-to-metaphor mapping
- `workspace` = “conscious”: currently selected and globally available state, not subjective awareness.
- `retrievable_memory` = “preconscious”: accessible but not currently loaded records.
- `model_priors + associations + inactive policies` = “unconscious”: processes not represented in workspace, not repression or hidden desire.
- `scheduler` = “ego”: resource allocation and arbitration.
- `appraisal_controller` = “id”: urgency/preference/novelty signals.
- `policy_gate` = “superego”: explicit constraints and consent rules.

#### Evaluation requirements
- Run LoCoMo and LongMemEval-style tests for single-hop and multi-hop recall, time, updates, contradiction handling, and abstention. — [LoCoMo code/data, 2024](https://github.com/snap-research/locomo); [LongMemEval code, 2025](https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval)
- Add Amelia-specific longitudinal tests for identity charter stability, relationship-boundary retention, false-memory correction, provenance display, graceful forgetting, and migration across local model versions.
- Measure fast-path latency and escalation rate separately from slow-path quality; dual-process work shows that conditional escalation is valuable only if the router is calibrated. — [Synergy-of-Thoughts, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02563)
- Test every conflict gate with adversarial cases and independent or executable validators; do not count ungrounded self-critique as verification. — [InferAct, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11843); [Self-verification limitations, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08115)
- Evaluate the neutral module names first. Only retain a psyche metaphor if it improves developer understanding or user experience without changing results or encouraging false scientific claims.

### Gaps
- Final module boundaries depend on Amelia’s current code, local model sizes, latency target, device constraints, privacy model, and tool permissions; those implementation facts were outside this research assignment.
- The literature does not settle whether autonomous memory evolution is worth its corruption risk. Amelia should begin with append-only events and reviewable derived memories, then ablate more autonomous consolidation.
